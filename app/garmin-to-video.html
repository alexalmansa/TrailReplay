Turn a Garmin Activity Into a 3D Route Video

Export GPX from Garmin Connect and use TrailReplay to animate the activity with 3D terrain, elevation, statistics, photos and video export.

  1. Open the activity in Garmin Connect.
  2. Choose Export to GPX.
  3. Import and customize the route in TrailReplay.
  4. Record the finished replay.
