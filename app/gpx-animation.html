Animate a GPX File on a 3D Map

TrailReplay turns a recorded GPX or KML route into an animated map video with terrain, elevation, live statistics, photos and annotations.

  1. Import one route or combine several tracks.
  2. Choose the map, camera and trail style.
  3. Add statistics, pictures or annotations.
  4. Export the GPX animation as video.
