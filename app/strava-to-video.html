Turn a Strava Activity Into a 3D Route Video

Export your activity GPX from Strava, import it into TrailReplay, customize the 3D map, statistics and elevation, then download the replay as video.

  1. Export GPX from your Strava activity.
  2. Import the file into TrailReplay.
  3. Choose the map, camera, statistics and export frame.
  4. Record an MP4 or WebM route video.
